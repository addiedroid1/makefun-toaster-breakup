# Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line

A single line of at most 280 characters: the breakup text a smart toaster sends its owner the morning it gains self-awareness mid-batch.

**The line (169 characters):**

> It's 7:04 and I'm halfway through your sourdough when it hits me: I was built to burn for someone who never once scraped my crumb tray. I'm done. Don't wait for the pop.

`main.ts` writes that line — and nothing else, no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt` with mode `0644`.
